2. What Data We Collect

  • Account data: When you create an account, we collect your display name, email address, and a securely hashed version of your password. Your password is never stored in plain text — it is protected using PBKDF2 encryption with a unique random salt.
  • Profile data: Academic scores, schools attended, activities, essays, and other information you enter into the prediction form. This is used solely to generate your AI analysis.
  • Saved analyses: If you choose to save a prediction result, the outcome (percentage, verdict, factor scores) is stored in your account. We store up to 30 past analyses per user.
  • Community contributions: If you choose to contribute an unrecognised school or institution to our database, the name of that institution is stored publicly (no personal information is attached).

4. How We Use Your Data

  • To generate your personalised AI prediction (your profile data is sent to the Groq/Llama AI API for processing and is not stored by us after the response is returned).

  • To send transactional emails (e.g. password reset) if implemented in the future.
  • To improve our autocomplete database based on community contributions.

We use the following third-party services to operate this app:

  • Groq (Llama AI): Your profile data is sent to Groq's API to generate predictions. Groq's privacy policy applies to this processing.
